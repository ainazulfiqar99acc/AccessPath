FORM NS-104 / EDUCATION SUPPORT

Student scholarship application

Complete all mandatory particulars. This demonstration does not determine eligibility or award funding. Documents are a readiness checklist; no files are collected.

Your details

Enter your full name as shown on your student record. Maximum 80 characters.

Use an email address where you can receive updates.

Your studies

Choose the level of the course you currently attend.

Enter your student reference. Use 4 to 20 letters, numbers or hyphens.

Funding details

Tell us whether you currently receive other education funding.

If you receive other funding, name the provider. Maximum 200 characters.

Describe what you want to learn and how this support would help. Use 20 to 600 characters. Do not include medical or financial account details.

Documentary prerequisites

  • Have your current student record ready. Do not upload it here.
  • Have proof of enrollment ready. Do not upload it here.

The source says applications are reviewed “in due course” but gives no review date. Ask the fictional fund administrator for clarification.
