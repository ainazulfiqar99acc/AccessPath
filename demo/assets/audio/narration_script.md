## 48.6-66.3 seconds: questions

## 67.0-80.2 seconds: statement

The same source limits apply to the statement. Requirements come from the source, without eligibility promises. The guide explains; you choose what to enter.

## 80.7-91.3 seconds: confirmation

Now review your answers. Nothing is sent until you explicitly confirm. Change an answer, and that confirmation becomes invalid. The decision stays with you.

## 94.5-102.5 seconds: receipt

A real browser fills the supported fictional portal and returns a local receipt. No real organization was contacted.

## 103.0-114.4 seconds: privacy

Activity records actions without repeating your answers. Delete the draft to remove its local data. Less decoding. A clear next step. Always your decision.
